# Section 06 — Traction & Metrics

**BigPawRides** | Pre-Seed | March 2026

---

## We Are Pre-Revenue. Here Is What We Have Validated

BigPawRides has not yet launched. There are zero customers, zero trips, and zero
revenue. We are raising €100K to create the traction that seed investors would
expect to see at the next round.

This is the honest starting point. What follows is the validation chain that
tells us the business is real, the gap is structural, and the timing is right.

---

## Validation Chain

### 1. The Problem Is Personal and Confirmed

Founder Roland Tritsch is a large dog owner living in Dublin. He has personally
been unable to book a standard taxi or ride-share for his dog — repeatedly, and
across multiple platforms. This is not a hypothesised problem. It is a lived
operational reality for the exact customer this business serves.

### 2. The Gap Is Structural, Not Accidental

Research conducted in March 2026 confirms:

- **Uber Pet is not available in Ireland** — the feature exists in select UK
  cities but has not been launched in the Irish market.
- **Bolt operates no pet tier** in Ireland or the Republic.
- **No purpose-built large-dog taxi service** was found on Gumtree.ie,
  DoneDeal.ie, or any Irish pet services marketplace.

The gap is not a temporary oversight. It reflects a structural mismatch between
the density of large dog ownership in Dublin (estimated 63,000 large dogs;
15,000–19,000 households with no transport solution) and the complete absence of
dedicated supply.

### 3. The Model Is Proven in Comparable Markets

The UK provides direct proof of concept at similar scale and pricing:

- **PetCabs (Bristol)** — purpose-built pet transport, operating profitably.
- **PetCars (London)** — large-dog specialist, similar trip economics.

Both operate at pricing comparable to the BigPawRides model (€30–45/trip). Both
are profitable. Neither has expanded to Ireland, leaving the Dublin market open.

### 4. The Founder Is the Right Person to Execute

Roland Tritsch is a serial entrepreneur with prior company-building experience.
He is not learning to start a business — he is applying that experience to a
market gap he has directly experienced. Execution credibility at pre-seed is a
primary investor signal; it is present here.

---

## What This €100K Raise Creates

This raise funds the first 12–18 months of operations. The milestones it is
designed to produce — which become the proof points for a seed round — are:

| Milestone                          | Target                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Completed trips in first 60 days   | 50+                          |
| Repeat booking rate within 90 days | 30%+                         |
| MRR by Month 6                     | €2,000–€3,000                |
| Net Promoter Score                 | 60+ (pet services benchmark) |
| Vet clinic referral partnerships   | 3+ established               |

These are not vanity metrics. They are the specific signals seed investors use
to evaluate whether a transport-side marketplace is working: throughput,
retention, and referral channel conversion. This raise is engineered to produce
exactly that evidence.
