# Section 04 — Product

## BigPawRides: The Ride Experience

BigPawRides is not an app. It is not a platform. It is a service — and the
product is the experience a large-dog owner receives from the moment they make
contact to the moment their dog arrives safely at the door.

For the 40% of Dublin dog owners with a large breed, getting their dog to a vet,
groomer, or boarding facility is a recurring, stressful, and often impossible
logistics problem. Standard taxis refuse. Family cars become anxious chaos.
BigPawRides removes that friction entirely.

---

## The Journey (5 Steps)

**1. Book** — Owner contacts via WhatsApp or phone. They share their dog's name,
breed, pickup and drop-off address, and the purpose of the trip. No app to
download. No account to create.

**2. Confirm** — Price and time are confirmed immediately. A reminder is sent
before the pickup window. The owner knows exactly what to expect.

**3. Collect** — The van arrives. A loading ramp deploys. The dog boards calmly
into a purpose-built aluminium transport box — not a folded-down back seat, not
a towel in the boot.

**4. Transport** — The environment is climate-controlled, non-slip, and
ventilated. There is no other unfamiliar animal in the vehicle. The driver is a
large-dog owner. Dogs travel calmly because the space was designed for them.

**5. Deliver** — The dog arrives. The owner is notified on departure and
arrival. Return trips are bookable at the time of the original booking — one
call handles the full round trip.

## The Vehicle

A used Transit Custom-size van converted to UK K9 transport specification — the
same standard used by police and working-dog handlers. Total cost: €20–28K (van
plus conversion), well within the €30–40K capital budget.

Conversion includes: aluminium transport box, non-slip flooring, drainage,
ventilation fan, and a loading ramp. Capacity: one to two large dogs (25–60 kg)
per trip.

---

## What Makes It Different

**Large-dog exclusive.** Not a generalist who occasionally accepts big dogs.
Every booking, every trip, is for large breeds only.

**Purpose-built vehicle.** The environment signals competence before the van
door opens. Owners see it once and trust it immediately.

**Founder-driver.** The same person every trip. Dogs and owners build a
relationship with a consistent, known face — not a rotating roster.

**Reliability guarantee.** No last-minute refusals. No "sorry, your dog is too
big." That promise, kept consistently, is the entire brand.

---

## Defensibility

Trust compounds in the Dublin pet community. Reviews spread through vet waiting
rooms, grooming salons, and neighbourhood Facebook groups faster than any paid
channel. The referral network being built with vets and groomers — structural
partnerships that take months to earn — creates a distribution moat that a
competitor cannot replicate quickly. Pet services consistently produce NPS
scores of 60–80; once an owner finds a solution that works, churn is near zero.
