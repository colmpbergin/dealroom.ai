## Assumptions (all clearly labeled)

### Revenue Assumptions

| Assumption                         | Value                                    | Rationale                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First paying customer              | Month 1                                  | Van purchased in setup month (M0)                                           |
| Average trip fare                  | €35                                      | Blended €25–€50 range; short trips €25, vet/long trips €50                  |
| Trips in Month 1                   | ~22 (5/week)                             | Conservative launch; word-of-mouth + vet referral seeding                   |
| Trip growth rate                   | +2 trips/week per month (months 1–9)     | Assumption: vet referral + Facebook group traction                          |
| Trip growth rate                   | +1–2 trips/week per month (months 10–18) | Assumption: repeat customers + word-of-mouth compounding                    |
| Fare inflation                     | +€1 average by Year 2                    | Modest premium uplift as brand established                                  |
| Repeat customer rate               | 60% within 90 days                       | Benchmark: pet services NPS 60–80; high loyalty category                    |
| No second revenue stream at launch | —                                        | Single operator; per-trip only; no subscriptions or B2B contracts at launch |
