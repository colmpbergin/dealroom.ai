# Target Customer

## Primary Customer Profile (ICP)

### B2C — Large Dog Owner in Dublin

- **Age range**: 28–65 (broad; primary focus 28–45 urban professionals)
- **Location**: Dublin city, inner suburbs, and outer suburbs
- **Income level**: Middle to upper-middle income; premium pet spending
- **Key behaviour**: Owns a large breed dog (Labrador, Golden Retriever, German
  Shepherd, Husky, Bernese, etc.); uses professional pet services (vet, groomer,
  daycare, boarding); either car-free or has a car unsuitable for dog transport
- **Willingness to pay**: High — already spends significantly on pet care;
  €25–€50 per trip is comparable to or cheaper than alternatives when they can
  even find one

## Three Key Segments

### Segment 1 — Urban Professional (Primary)

- City-dweller, 28–45, rents in Dublin city or inner suburbs
- No car, or car not suitable for a large dog
- Uses professional pet services regularly but struggles with transport
- Highest frequency, highest immediate pain

### Segment 2 — Suburban Family

- Dublin suburbs, has a car but it's unsuitable (hatchback, luxury car, family
  SUV they don't want covered in dog hair)
- Regular groomer + vet visits, may use daycare
- Lower pain intensity but high frequency potential

### Segment 3 — Elderly Owner

- 60+, large companion dog (often Labrador, Golden Retriever)
- Cannot physically manage transporting a large dog
- Cannot drive, or drives but cannot manage the dog in the car
- High willingness to pay for a trusted, reliable service

## The Pain They Feel

"I booked an Uber to take my dog to the vet. Three drivers cancelled when they
saw the size of him. I was 20 minutes late to a post-surgery appointment."

"My groomer is 8km away. I don't have a car. I can't get any taxi to take a
60kg dog. I ended up cancelling."

"I love having a big dog but the logistics are exhausting. Every vet trip is
a production."

## How They Currently Solve It

- **Ask friends / family**: Unreliable, creates social debt
- **Owner drives themselves**: Not always possible (no car, unsuitable car,
  anxious/injured dog they can't safely handle alone)
- **Standard pet taxis**: Often refuse large breeds or don't have appropriate
  vehicles
- **Delay / cancel**: Many simply delay vet visits or cancel grooming — a
  health and wellbeing cost to the dog

## Why They Switch to BigPawRides

- **Guaranteed acceptance**: Never refused at the door
- **Purpose-built**: Right vehicle, right restraints, right driver
- **Recurring trust**: Once they've trusted us with their dog, they come back
  every time — no need to search for alternatives again

## Customer Acquisition

**Primary channels**:

1. **Vet clinic referrals**: Vets hear this complaint constantly — natural
   referral partner
2. **Grooming salon referrals**: Same pain point, same referral opportunity
3. **Dublin dog owner Facebook / WhatsApp groups**: Highly trusted local
   recommendations
4. **Instagram / TikTok**: Large dog content is inherently shareable;
   behind-the-scenes of happy dogs builds organic reach
5. **Word of mouth**: Tight-knit large dog owner community; one great
   experience = multiple referrals
