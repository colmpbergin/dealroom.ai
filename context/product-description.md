# Product Description

## Core Product

BigPawRides is a purpose-built large-dog taxi service. The product is the
trip experience: safe, stress-free, door-to-door transport for large dogs in
Dublin.

## How It Works

1. **Book**: Customer contacts BigPawRides (phone / WhatsApp / booking form)
   and schedules a trip — specifying dog breed, size, pickup/dropoff, and
   purpose (vet, groomer, etc.)
2. **Confirm**: Trip is confirmed with price and time. Customer receives a
   reminder before pickup.
3. **Collect**: Driver arrives at pickup location in the purpose-built van.
   Dog is loaded safely — ramp or assisted entry, appropriate crating or
   restraint for the breed.
4. **Transport**: Dog is transported calmly and safely. Driver is experienced
   with large dogs. Climate-controlled, clean, non-slip environment.
5. **Deliver**: Dog arrives at destination. Owner is notified. Return trip
   bookable at time of initial booking.

## Vehicle

**Type**: Van or large SUV (purpose-built)

**Features**:

- Crating options suitable for 30–60kg+ breeds
- Non-slip surfaces, easy-clean lining
- Entry ramp or lowered floor for older / post-surgery dogs
- Adequate ventilation and climate control
- Cleaning supplies always on board

## What Makes It Different

- **Large-dog exclusive**: Not a generalist pet taxi that sometimes takes big
  dogs — exclusively built for large breeds
- **Purpose-built vehicle**: Not a standard car or van used for multiple purposes
- **Dog-familiar driver**: The founder is a large dog owner — a meaningful
  difference in how dogs respond to the experience
- **Reliability guarantee**: No risk of being refused at the door (the #1 pain
  point with ride-share)

## Defensibility / Moat

- **Brand trust**: In a high-stakes service (transporting a beloved pet), trust
  is everything. Early brand-building in Dublin creates a sticky reputation
  that is very hard for a new entrant to displace quickly.
- **Vet + groomer relationships**: Referral partnerships with Dublin vets and
  groomers create a distribution moat that takes time to build.
- **Repeat customers**: High retention — once an owner trusts a driver with
  their dog, they come back every time.

## Product Roadmap

- **Near term (0–6 months)**: Launch operations; phone/WhatsApp bookings;
  build first 20–30 repeat customers
- **Medium term (6–18 months)**: Simple website with online booking form
  (third-party scheduling tool); establish 3–5 vet/groomer referral
  partnerships; reach operational break-even
- **Long term**: Mobile app, fleet expansion (additional drivers + vehicles),
  multi-city replication (Cork, Galway, Belfast)
