# Fundraising Stage Context

> This is one of the most important context files. Claude uses this to calibrate

> what traction evidence to seek, how to frame the investor narrative, and what

## What Stage Are We Raising?

- [ ] **Pre-Seed** — little to no revenue, early prototype or concept, raising
      primarily on team + vision
- [ ] **Seed** — early revenue or strong traction signals, raising to prove
      product-market fit and unit economics

## Key Differences Claude Will Apply Based on Your Answer

| Element                | Pre-Seed Mode                                           | Seed Mode                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Financial model        | 1-sheet, 12–18mo cash flow + 3yr directional            | Multi-tab, monthly 3yr + scenarios                 |
| Traction bar           | Waitlist, pilots, LOIs, interviews OK                   | MRR, NRR, CAC/LTV, cohort retention expected       |
| Missing data           | Replace with benchmarks + assumptions (clearly labeled) | Must use actuals; gaps are red flags               |
| Team section weight    | Highest priority — investors bet on founders            | Important but traction speaks louder               |
| Competitive section    | Positioning narrative OK                                | Must show win/loss data and differentiated metrics |
| Deal room size         | 5–10 documents                                          | 15–25+ documents                                   |
| Financial share format | PDF summary only                                        | PDF summary; full model available on request       |

## The "Why Now" Argument

> This is the #1 most commonly missing element in pitch decks (absent from 75%
> per research). What has changed in the world — technologically, regulatorily,
> demographically, or culturally — that makes NOW the right moment for this
> company to exist?

[e.g., "The shift to remote work created X million new users of Y type of
tool...", "Recent regulation change means companies must now...", "LLMs just
made it possible to do X at 1/10th the cost..."]
