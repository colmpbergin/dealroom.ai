## The "Why Now" Argument

- **Post-COVID pet ownership surge**: Dublin and Ireland saw record dog adoption
  rates in 2020–2022. The large dog population has grown substantially, creating
  a larger addressable market than existed 5 years ago.
- **Ride-share maturation**: As Uber/Bolt/Free Now dominate Dublin transport,
  drivers have become more risk-averse and policy-compliant — making large-dog
  refusals more systematic, not less.
- **Premium pet services boom**: Irish pet owners now spend more per pet than
  ever before. The willingness to pay for specialist pet services (not just vet
  care) is at an all-time high.
- **Urban density + car-free living**: More Dublin residents live car-free in
  apartments. The logistical gap for large dog transport has never been wider.
