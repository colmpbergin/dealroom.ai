> get asked that isn't clearly answered in the data room belongs here. Claude

> Appendix FAQ.

> of a deal room. Start it before your first meeting and add to it after every
> conversation.

3. Note your answer

   the data room

`/dealroom:build-section` to ensure the data room proactively answers these
questions.

## The 9 Questions Every Investor Will Ask

These come from research across 40+ VC firms. Every data room must answer all 9.
Check off each one as it becomes clearly addressed in the data room.

- [ ] **Why will you win?** (not just why you're good — why do you specifically
      beat everyone else?)
- [ ] **Why now?** (what's changed in the world that makes this the right
      moment?)
- [ ] **Why this team?** (founder-market fit — why are YOU the right people?)
- [ ] **What's the evidence this works?** (traction, validation, proof points)
- [ ] **What are the unit economics?** (CAC, LTV, margins — or benchmarked
      assumptions)
- [ ] **What's the exit?** (implied or explicit — what does a great outcome look
      like in 7-10 years?)
- [ ] **What are the risks?** (acknowledged honestly — investors respect
      honesty, distrust evasion)
- [ ] **What specifically does this capital accomplish?** (milestones, not just
      spend categories)
- [ ] **When do you raise again and at what metrics?** (what does Series A
      readiness look like?)

## Comprehensive Investor Question Bank

_Source: Startup fundraising research — use to pre-populate answers_

### Overview Questions

- What does the company do?
- What is unique about the company?
- What big problem does it solve?
- How big is the market opportunity?
- How big can the company get?

### Marketing & GTM Questions

- Who is your target market?
- What do you anticipate the customer acquisition costs to be?
- What is the typical sales cycle between initial customer contact and closing a
  sale?
- What is your pricing model?
- What is your product roadmap for 1–3 years?
- Will you be relying on third-party distribution channels? Are they
  sufficiently incentivised?
- How will you employ social media, paid search, content marketing, PR?

### Financial Metrics Investors Will Ask For

- Monthly burn rate
- Projected growth in revenues
- Gross margin (target: 40%+ for VC-backable)
- Lifetime value (LTV) of a customer
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- LTV:CAC ratio (should be >3:1)
- EBITDA
- How long to profitability?
- How much additional capital will need to be raised, and when?
- Three-year projections + key assumptions
- What % of stock option pool is set aside for employees?
- What are the factors that limit faster growth?

### Risk Questions

- What are the principal risks to the business?
- What legal risks do you have?
- Do you have any regulatory risks?
- Are there any product liability risks?

### Market Size Questions

- What is the actual addressable market?
- What percentage of the market do you plan to get, over what period?
- How did you arrive at the industry sales figure and growth rate?
- Why does your company have high growth potential?

### Product & Differentiation Questions

- Who are the company's competitors? (Never say "none")
- What gives your company a competitive advantage?
- What advantages does your competition have over you?
- What are the barriers to entry?
- How easy will it be to replicate the technology?
- What have you learned from early versions of the product?
- What are the two or three key features you plan to add?
- Do you have any favorable customer reviews?

### IP Questions

- What key intellectual property does the company have (patents, copyrights,
  trade secrets, trademarks)?
- Would any prior employers of a team member have a potential claim to the IP?
- How was the IP developed?

### Team Questions

- Have members of the team set up a company before?
- Have members of the team worked together before?
- What relevant domain experience does the team have?
- What key additions to the team are needed short term?
- What motivates the founders?
- How do you plan to scale the team in the next 1–3 years?

### Financing Round Questions

- How much is being raised in this round?
- What is the company's desired pre-money valuation?
- Will existing investors participate in the round?
- What is the planned use of proceeds?
- What milestones will the financing get you to?

### Exit Strategy Questions

- What is the exit strategy?
- Who could potentially acquire the company and why?
- What is the realistic timeframe for an exit?
- What exit multiples are reasonable based on comparables?

### Traction Questions (for pre-seed: qualitative OK; for seed: actuals required)

- Do you have a beta or MVP?
- Any initial or pilot customers, especially brand names?
- Any strategic partnerships?
- Customer testimonials?
- Admission into accelerators (YC, Techstars, etc.)?

---

> "Don't worry if you can't answer all the questions. Investors just want to
> know you are aware of them. Bring the list to meetings, highlight what you can
> answer, what you're working on, and give a timeline for the rest. Transparency
> and awareness = credibility."
