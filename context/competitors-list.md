## The "Do Nothing" Option

Large dog owners delay or cancel vet appointments, grooming visits, and daycare
trips because transport is too difficult. The "status quo" is **frustrated
inaction** — which is also the strongest argument for BigPawRides.

## Our Competitive Positioning

BigPawRides is not a better generic pet taxi. It is the **only** purpose-built,
large-dog-exclusive taxi service in Dublin. The positioning is:

- **Exclusivity**: We only do large dogs. This is a feature, not a limitation.
- **Purpose-built**: The right vehicle, the right restraints, the right driver.
- **Reliability**: You will never be refused at the door.

## Why We Win

1. **Guaranteed acceptance** — no other service in Dublin can promise this for
   large breeds
2. **Purpose-built vehicle and experience** — not an afterthought or ad-hoc
   solution
3. **Founder is the customer** — a large dog owner driving a van built for large
   dogs is the most credible possible product for this audience
