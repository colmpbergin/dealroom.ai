# Legal & Corporate Readiness

> Investors commonly kill or delay deals due to legal gaps discovered in
> diligence. This file helps Claude audit your legal readiness and flag gaps
> before investors find them. One of the 7 most common deal-killers is missing
> IP assignment documentation. Fill this out honestly — gaps flagged early can
> be fixed; gaps found in diligence kill deals.
