# Business Info

## Company Name

BigPawRides

## One-Line Description

The dedicated taxi service for large dogs in Dublin — safe, purpose-built
transport for breeds that ride-share and standard pet taxis routinely refuse.

## Mission

To make owning a large dog in an urban environment as stress-free as possible —
starting with transport, the most overlooked and underserved part of large-dog
ownership.

## What We Do

Large dog owners in Dublin face a genuine transport crisis: Uber and Bolt
drivers routinely refuse large breeds, standard pet taxis focus on small dogs,
and many urban residents don't own cars suitable for a 40–60kg animal.
BigPawRides fills this gap with a purpose-built, owner-operated van service
designed exclusively for large dogs.

The service is simple: book a trip, we collect your dog, transport them safely
to the vet / groomer / kennel / wherever they need to go, and deliver them back.
The driver is a large dog owner — dogs respond to that.

At launch, BigPawRides is intentionally simple: one operator, one vehicle, one
city. The goal is to prove the unit economics, build a loyal repeat customer
base, and establish the brand before scaling the fleet.

## Products / Services

- Per-trip large-dog transport (vet, grooming, daycare, boarding, airport)
- Bookable via phone / WhatsApp at launch; booking form website to follow

## Key Context for Investors

- This is a service business, not a platform. The founder drives. This is a
  deliberate choice: quality control, dog familiarity, and brand trust are built
  by the founder directly.
- The model is proven in other cities (pet taxi services exist); the gap is the
  **large dog focus** and **purpose-built vehicle** — neither of which the
  Dublin market currently has.
- First-mover advantage in a clearly underserved niche with recurring demand.
