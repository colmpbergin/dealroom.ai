# Section 09 — Go-to-Market

**BigPawRides** | Pre-Seed | Dublin | March 2026

---

## The Wedge

One customer. One city zone. One problem.

Urban Dublin professionals aged 28–42, renting in D1–D8, who own large-breed
dogs and have no car. This is the highest-pain, highest-frequency segment —
these owners cannot improvise a solution. They cannot borrow a neighbour's SUV
for every vet appointment. They are already Googling and finding nothing. That
gap is the entry point.

Everything in Month 1 is aimed at this segment only. Suburban families and
elderly owners are real future segments; they are not the wedge.

---

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Evidence Base | Est. CAC | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vet clinic referrals | UK pet taxis cite vets as #1 referral source; vets hear "I couldn't get there" cancellations directly; 50–70 Dublin practices in target zones | €0–€30 | Primary — launch week |
| Grooming salon referrals | Groomers hear the transport complaint daily; reciprocal model requires no cash outlay | €0–€5 | Primary — parallel to vets |
| Dublin dog Facebook / WhatsApp groups | 5,000–20,000 combined members; highest-trust channel for local service discovery; zero cost to reach | €0–€15 | Primary — launch burst |
| Word of mouth | Pet services NPS 60–80; 79% of pet owners recommend a loved service to 3+ friends (Podium, 2023) | €0 marginal | Highest LTV; compounds over time |
| Google My Business (free listing) | Zero current competitors ranking for "pet taxi Dublin large dog"; free; ranks fast in a zero-competition query | €0–€20 | Set up immediately |
| Instagram / TikTok organic | Large-dog content drives 3–5x average engagement; van setup and calm transport are inherently shareable | €5–€40 | Secondary; build from day 1 |

Paid social and Google Ads are deliberately absent from Month 1. Cash goes in
only after reviews exist and organic conversion is proven (Month 3–4 at
earliest).

---

## Month 1 Launch Plan

1. **Vet visits** — Call on 10 highest-volume practices in D1–D8, D12, D15.
   Leave 20 referral cards each. Speak directly to the practice manager.
   Vets benefit: fewer cancelled appointments, a trusted solution to offer
   distressed owners.

2. **Community seeding** — Post a genuine founder introduction in the top 3–5
   Dublin dog Facebook groups. Offer 5 pilot trips at a discounted rate in
   exchange for honest written feedback. No hard sell; social trust is the
   asset.

3. **Google My Business** — Create the free listing in week 1. With no
   competing entries, even a handful of early reviews produce first-page
   organic placement for high-intent searches.

4. **Content launch** — Film the first test run: van setup, dog loading, calm
   transport. Publish to Instagram and TikTok. This is brand-building and
   conversion evidence simultaneously.

## The Referral Flywheel

Vet encounters a client who cannot transport their large dog to appointments
→ refers them to BigPawRides → BigPawRides delivers a calm, safe trip → customer
tells their dog group and leaves a Google review → new customers find the listing
→ they attend the same vet → vet refers more clients.

Each completed trip seeds the next three acquisition channels at zero marginal cost.

The LTV:CAC ratio improves as word-of-mouth compounds and paid channels are
added only after organic proof-of-concept. The €21,000 annual recurring base
is the core of the break-even thesis.

## What We Are Not Doing Yet

- **Paid social** — organic content starts day 1; cash investment follows
  reviews (Month 3–4)
- **Google Ads** — GMB listing first; paid search after conversion data exists
- **B2B groomer white-label partnerships** — piloted at Month 3–4 once the
  service model is proven

Sequencing matters. Spending on paid acquisition before social proof exists
raises CAC and depresses conversion. The referral channels are free, trusted,
and already warm to the problem. That is where Month 1 capital goes.
