# Section 05 — Operations & Technology

## No Engineering. By Design.

BigPawRides is a service business. There is no custom software at launch — and
that is not an oversight. It is a deliberate cost advantage. Building technology
before proving demand is how startups burn through pre-seed capital without
learning anything. The right order is: validate the model, build the routes,
earn the referrals — then automate what is working. At this stage, the founder's
time is worth more dispatching rides and calling vet clinics than managing a
sprint backlog.

Total software overhead at launch: under €70 per month.

## Operational Stack

| Layer | Tool | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online presence | Squarespace website | ~€20/mo |
| Booking + payments | Calendly + Stripe | ~€30/mo |
| Trip routing | Google Maps | Free |
| Customer comms | WhatsApp Business | Free |
| Accounting + VAT | FreeAgent | ~€20/mo |
| Marketing | Instagram + TikTok | Free |

Every tool listed is off-the-shelf, proven, and replaceable. None of them
creates dependency or lock-in. The business can be operational within 48 hours
of funding.

## Where the Moat Actually Lives

The competitive advantage of BigPawRides is not technology. It is brand trust,
vehicle quality, and referral relationships with Dublin's veterinary clinics,
groomers, and dog trainers. These are relationships that take time to build and
are genuinely difficult to replicate. A competitor cannot copy a Squarespace
site; they cannot copy three years of goodwill with a vet practice. That is
where the defensibility is.

---

## Operational Disciplines (What Replaces Engineering Process)

- **Service quality standard**: Every ride logged — pick-up time, drop-off
  time, customer satisfaction note. Manual at launch; structured once volume
  warrants it.
- **Safety protocol**: Vehicle inspection checklist before every shift. Crates
  secured, restraints checked, ventilation confirmed. Not optional.
- **Vehicle maintenance schedule**: Serviced every 10,000 km or 6 months,
  whichever comes first. Clean cabin is a brand signal, not a hygiene box-tick.
- **Referral tracking**: Every new customer asked how they heard about
  BigPawRides. Source tracked in a simple spreadsheet. This is the early demand
  intelligence that will eventually inform channel spend.

## Technology Roadmap

Technology gets built when it is justified by demand — in this order:

1. **Booking app** (iOS + Android) — once manual booking volume exceeds what
   Calendly handles cleanly, or when repeat customers request it.
2. **Real-time tracking** — owners can see where their dog is during the ride.
   High-value feature for anxious owners; builds trust before a second booking
   is made.
3. **Driver dispatch** — when the fleet grows beyond one van and one driver,
   routing and assignment needs to be systematised.
4. **Fleet management + scheduling** — at 3+ vehicles, manual scheduling creates
   operational risk. This is the point at which custom software replaces
   spreadsheets.

Each stage is funded by the revenue from the previous stage. No speculative
engineering spend.
