# BigPawRides — Investment Memo

**Date:** 2026-03-21 | **Stage:** Pre-Seed | **Ask:** €100,000 SAFE at €1.8M
post-money cap

## Company Purpose

BigPawRides is a purpose-built large-dog taxi service in Dublin, Ireland — the
only guaranteed large-breed transport solution for the 15,000 households that
ride-share platforms and standard pet taxis routinely refuse.

## The Problem

Large dog owners in Dublin (63,000 large dogs estimated in Dublin metro) have no
reliable transport option. Uber Pet is not available in Ireland. Bolt has no pet
tier. Licensed taxi drivers are under no NTA obligation to carry animals.
Standard pet taxis focus on small breeds. The result: ~15,000 households face
cancelled vet appointments, delayed grooming, and borrowed cars for every trip.
Economic cost: €200–€600 per household per year in disrupted pet care.

## Why Now

Five converging conditions have peaked in 2024–2026:

1. The COVID dog adoption cohort (2020–2022) is now 3–5 years old at peak
   veterinary and grooming need.
2. Ride-share driver refusals have become systematic as Uber and Bolt have
   professionalised Dublin's private hire market.
3. Premium pet service spending in Ireland is at a record high (Euromonitor,
   2025).
4. Dublin's car-free household rate is rising with urban density.
5. No competitor has moved to fill the gap despite conditions existing
   since 2022.

## The Solution

BigPawRides operates a purpose-built van (Transit Custom-size, K9 conversion
specification) driven by the founder — a large dog owner himself. The service
guarantees large-breed acceptance on every booking. €25–€50 per trip. Bookable
via WhatsApp at launch. Target market: Dublin city and inner suburbs.

## Market Opportunity

- **TAM**: ~€18M/year (all large-dog transport in Ireland; bottom-up)
- **SAM**: ~€5.25M/year (Dublin metro; 30,000 addressable households × 5 trips ×
  €35)
- **SOM**: ~€70K/year by Year 3 (single operator; 1.3% of SAM — highly credible)
- **Growth**: Global pet transport CAGR 9.2% (Verified Market Research, 2024);
  Irish pet care 6–9%/year (Euromonitor, 2025)

## Traction

- Problem confirmed: zero purpose-built large-dog taxi services found on any
  Irish marketplace (Gumtree.ie, DoneDeal.ie, Trustpilot IE — zero results,
  March 2026)
- Uber Pet not available in Ireland; Bolt has no pet tier in Dublin (both

## Business Model

Per-trip fee at €25–€50 (blended €35 avg). Break-even at 99 trips/month
(~23/week). LTV per active customer: ~€1,225 over 5 years. Blended CAC: <€30
(vet referrals + Facebook groups). LTV:CAC ratio: >40:1.

## Why This Team

Roland Tritsch is a serial entrepreneur who IS the customer — a large dog owner
in Dublin who experienced systematic ride-share refusals firsthand. Serial
founder background (company formation, fundraising, operations, customer
development) materially reduces execution risk for a solo operator raise. The
founder-driver model is the product differentiator: quality control is
impossible to delegate at launch.

## Competitive Landscape

No direct competitor with a public booking channel, purpose-built vehicle, or
large-dog guarantee exists in Dublin. Uber, Bolt, and Free Now all lack pet
products in Ireland. The primary competition is the status quo (owner drives
themselves), which fails for ~30% of Dublin City households with no car. UK
comparables (PetCabs, PetCars) prove the model in comparable cities but have not
entered Ireland.

## Risks

1. **Demand ramp risk**: Transport demand may ramp slower than projected,
   delaying break-even. Mitigation: €18K buffer + 24-month runway; downside
   model reaches break-even at Month 20+ but never runs out of cash.
2. **Solo founder / key-person dependency**: If the founder is unavailable,
   operations stop. Mitigation: intentional for months 1–12 (quality control
   requires it); second driver is the first seed-stage hire.
3. **Commercial pet transport licensing**: No Ireland-specific licence
   requirement has been confirmed. Mitigation: engage Irish solicitor before
   launch; flag proactively to investors.
