# BigPawRides — One-Pager

**Date:** 2026-03-21 | **Stage:** Pre-Seed | **Ask:** €100,000 SAFE at €1.8M
post-money cap

## What We Do

BigPawRides is Dublin's first purpose-built taxi service for large dogs —
guaranteeing large-breed acceptance on every booking.

## The Problem

63,000 large dogs live in the Dublin metro, yet no ride-share or pet taxi
service reliably accepts them: Uber Pet is unavailable in Ireland, Bolt has no
pet tier, and licensed taxi drivers face no NTA obligation to carry animals.
~15,000 households face cancelled vet appointments, delayed grooming, and
borrowed cars for every single trip — at a cost of €200–€600 per year per
household.

## The Solution

A purpose-built K9-converted van driven by the founder, with guaranteed
large-breed acceptance on every booking. Trips are priced at €25–€50 (blended
€35), bookable via WhatsApp at launch, serving Dublin city and inner suburbs.

## Market

- **TAM**: ~€18M/year (all large-dog transport in Ireland; bottom-up)
- **SAM**: ~€5.25M/year (Dublin metro; 30,000 households × 5 trips × €35)
- **SOM**: ~€70K/year by Year 3 (1.3% of SAM as a single-operator service —
  highly credible)

## Team

Roland Tritsch is a serial entrepreneur and large dog owner in Dublin who
experienced the problem firsthand — repeatedly. His founder-driver model is not
a stop-gap; it is the quality guarantee that makes the service defensible at
launch.

## The Ask
