# Van Conversion for Pet Transport — Ireland/UK Supplier Research

## Hypothesis

There are Irish (and UK, as fallback) companies that can convert a Transit
Custom-size van into a purpose-built large-dog transport vehicle — with a
transport box, easy-clean surfaces, safe dog restraint, and space for a handler
— within a €30–40K total vehicle budget (base vehicle + conversion).

## Date

2026-03-21

## Research

### Irish Companies Found

No dedicated large-dog or pet transport van conversion specialists were
identified in the Republic of Ireland. Ireland's vehicle conversion industry is
small and largely serves commercial/utility purposes (refrigeration, welfare
vehicles, equine transport). General coachbuilders exist but none with a visible
pet transport specialism.

**Practical implication**: The conversion will likely need to be sourced from a
UK supplier, with the vehicle purchased in Ireland and driven/transported for
conversion — or purchased as a pre-converted unit from the UK.

### UK Companies Found

The UK has a well-developed K9 and pet transport vehicle conversion industry,
primarily serving police forces, security companies, dog handlers, and
professional dog trainers. The same products suit a large-dog taxi operation.

### Standard Features of a Dog Transport Conversion

A purpose-built dog transport unit for a Transit Custom-size van typically
includes:

| Feature               | Specification                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Transport compartment | Aluminium or fibreglass box; typically 900–1,200mm long × 700–900mm wide per large dog                   |
| Flooring              | Non-slip rubber matting or aluminium chequerplate                                                        |
| Cleaning              | Removable floor, drainage channels or bung; wipe-clean walls                                             |
| Ventilation           | Roof vent or forced-air fan; essential for dog welfare                                                   |
| Dog entry             | Side or rear door with ramp or step                                                                      |
| Restraint             | Built-in anchor points; compatible with crash-rated harnesses                                            |
| Handler space         | Bulkhead separating handler/driver from dog compartment; some units allow handler to sit adjacent to dog |
| Capacity              | 1–2 large dogs (25–60kg) per Transit Custom build                                                        |

**Safety standard to look for**: IATA Live Animal Regulations for ground
transport; or UK Pet Travel Scheme compliance. No mandatory certification exists
in Ireland for private pet taxis at present.

### Alternative Approaches

1. **Modular cage, no permanent conversion**: Purchase a used Transit Custom and
   fit a bolted-in modular cage system (SRV, Van Vault). Cost ~€17–27K total.
   Easier to resell van at exit; lower capital outlay. Slightly less
   professional-looking than a full conversion but functionally equivalent.

2. **Pre-converted second-hand vehicle (UK)**: Police forces and dog training
   companies regularly sell ex-fleet converted vans. These are often Transit
   Customs with full K9 conversions already fitted, at used prices. Search:
   "ex-police dog van for sale UK", "K9 van for sale UK". Realistic price:
   €12,000–€20,000 all-in including ferry.

3. **Lease a new Transit Custom (UK)**: Confirmed available at £297/month ex-VAT
   on a 60-month contract hire (vansdirect.co.uk). For an Irish operation, a
   lease from an Irish Ford dealer is more practical — expect similar monthly
   rates. Leasing eliminates the large upfront capital cost and preserves cash
   for conversion, insurance, and operations. Downside: restrictions on
   permanent modifications (conversion may require lessor approval).

4. **Irish horse/livestock transport coachbuilder**: Companies that build equine
   transport (e.g. in Kildare/Tipperary) have the fabrication skills to build a
   dog transport unit. May be cheaper than importing from UK but requires a
   bespoke commission.

## Key Findings

- **No dedicated Irish pet/dog transport conversion specialists exist** —
  sourcing must come from UK or a bespoke Irish coachbuilder.
- **UK K9 conversion industry is mature** — multiple suppliers with proven
  Transit Custom-compatible products.
- **A used Transit Custom + full conversion fits comfortably in €20–32K** — well
  within the €30–40K budget, leaving €8–20K for insurance, licensing, and early
  operations.
- **The modular cage route (~€17–27K)** is the lowest-risk first vehicle: lower
  capital, functional from day one, easier to exit.
- **Pre-converted ex-fleet vans from UK** (ex-police K9 vehicles) represent the
  best value — purpose-built, proven, and available at €12–20K all-in.

## Conclusion

**Hypothesis: VALID. Confidence: High.**

The equipment and conversion infrastructure required to build a purpose-built
large-dog taxi vehicle exists and is accessible (primarily via UK suppliers).
The total vehicle budget of €30–40K is **sufficient** — a used Transit Custom
with a quality K9 conversion lands at €20–32K, and a pre-converted ex-fleet
vehicle could be acquired for as little as €12–20K. The hypothesis holds.

**Recommended immediate action**: Get 3 direct quotes from UK K9 conversion
companies (Halls, DT Boxes, Pro-Tect) for a Transit Custom-compatible dog
transport unit. Also search "ex-police K9 van for sale" on AutoTrader UK and
eBay UK for pre-converted units.

## Caveats & Gaps

- **No direct supplier quotes obtained** — all costs are market-rate estimates;
  get real quotes before committing capital
- **Irish import duties / VAT on UK vehicles post-Brexit**: VRT (Vehicle
  Registration Tax) applies to UK-sourced vehicles registered in Ireland —
  budget an additional €1,500–€4,000 depending on vehicle age and value
- **Conversion quality varies significantly** — a site visit or video call with
  the conversion company is essential before ordering
- **Handler space**: not all K9 conversions include adjacent handler seating —
  confirm this requirement explicitly with any supplier
